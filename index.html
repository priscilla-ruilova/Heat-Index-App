What is a heat index?

Heat index takes into account the humidity and the temperature to give you the actual temperature that the human body feels. Our bodies sweat to regulate our body temperatures but if it's so hot that our sweat is not able to evaporate, our body's temparature will not regualte and we can begin to experience heat-related illnesses. See below for recommendations depending on the heat index!
